Hi, I'm Jason. I work in IT by day, which mostly consists of yelling and shaking my fist at different [clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing). Outside of work, I'm a left-handed, coffee-fueled geriatric millennial with a weakness for video games, an unreasonable memory for obscure movie trivia, and the occasional short story no one asked for.

This blog is "out of scope" on purpose. It's where I put the technology-related posts that don't fit anywhere else — documentation, how-tos, notes from experiments with new tech, and the lessons I pick up along the way. Not my day job, not my personal life, just the stuff in between.
